Data sources

River flow is modelled, not measured at a gauge: Copernicus GloFAS river discharge (5 km grid) served by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Forecast and history come from the same model so thresholds and forecasts are comparable. Rain warnings: Met Éireann. Resources and notes: the council sheet.

Met Éireann rain warning guide. Yellow: 20–30 mm in 6 h, 30–40 mm in 12 h, or 30–50 mm in 24 h. Orange: 30–50 mm in 6 h, 40–60 mm in 12 h, or 50–80 mm in 24 h. Red: more than 50 mm in 6 h, 60 mm in 12 h or 80 mm in 24 h. Warnings are issued up to 48 hours ahead. River Watch counts only rain warnings; orange or red wind, thunderstorm or other warnings are shown on the town card but change nothing.
